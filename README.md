## About

Lumière is a concept landing page for a premium private hospital in Bengaluru. Most hospital websites read like a directory — departments, forms, a wall of text. I wanted this one to feel closer to a boutique clinic's site: a slow, deliberate preloader that spells out the name letter by letter, a warm cream-and-sage palette instead of the usual clinical white-and-blue, and a serif display font that leans editorial rather than institutional. The goal was to make "getting a checkup" feel less like an errand and more like booking something you'd actually look forward to.

## What's on the page

- **Philosophy** — the clinic's approach to care, stated plainly
- **Pillars** — the core commitments (precision, privacy, continuity of care)
- **Checkups** — the comprehensive health screening packages on offer
- **Facilities** — a look at the physical space and equipment
- **Centers** — specialty departments and centers of excellence
- **Specialists** — the doctors, with real credentialing detail (AIIMS, Cleveland Clinic, MD Anderson fellowships)
- **Booking** — appointment request with a direct call option

## Why I built it this way

The preloader spells out "Lumière" letter by letter before the page reveals — a small, deliberate delay instead of a spinner. For a premium hospital brand, that half-second of anticipation does more for the "this is a considered, high-end place" feeling than any hero image could. It's the kind of detail that costs almost nothing in load time but changes the first impression entirely.

Doctor credentials list real institutions (AIIMS, Cleveland Clinic, MD Anderson) as fellowship/training affiliations rather than claiming the hospital itself is affiliated with them — a distinction that matters for a healthcare-adjacent site where credibility claims need to be precise, even in a concept build.
