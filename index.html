— Two Doors, One Continuum

A hospital & clinic, woven into one practice.

Outpatient consultation, day procedures, advanced imaging, and inpatient care are not separate buildings here. They are one medical record, one team, one philosophy — joined by a corridor, not a referral.

01 — Clinic

For the everyday.

Walk in for a consult, leave with answers. Same-day diagnostics, executive health screens, vaccines, second opinions, and chronic disease management — under one roof.

  • Walk-in & scheduled consultations, 7 days a week
  • On-site laboratory with results in under 4 hours
  • 3T MRI, 640-slice CT, digital mammography, ultrasound
  • Executive & preventive health programs
  • Paediatric, women’s health, geriatric wings
02 — Hospital

For the complex.

When care requires depth, the hospital is a floor above. 312 beds, 14 operating theatres, hybrid cath-lab, NICU, and 24/7 emergency — all staffed by the same consultants you saw downstairs.

  • 312 inpatient beds including 64 critical-care
  • 14 modular operating theatres with robotic surgery
  • Cardiac catheterisation & electrophysiology lab
  • Level-3 NICU & paediatric intensive care
  • 24/7 trauma, stroke, and chest-pain pathways

— The Patient Journey

From your first hello to your last follow-up.

Care is choreographed. Your navigator knows your case, your consultant knows your history, and your discharge plan is written before you arrive.

— Step 01

Concierge intake

One call, one message. A dedicated navigator takes your history, goals, and language preference — usually within 30 minutes.

~ 30 min
— Step 02

Consult & plan

Same-day or virtual consult with the senior consultant. Investigations ordered before you leave. Transparent cost, in writing.

~ 45 min
— Step 03

Diagnosis & treatment

Imaging, lab, day-care, or admission — all on the same campus, the same day, with results reviewed in multidisciplinary board.

Same day
— Step 04

Recovery & follow-up

Discharge plan, home care, teleconsults, and a 90-day navigator check-in. Your record follows you, in one place, forever.

90 days
